Community guidelines

We want real, useful conversation—not abuse. By posting or replying, you agree to:

  • Be respectful. No insults, threats, hate speech, harassment, or profanity.
  • Discuss ideas, not attack people. Disagreement is welcome when it stays civil.
  • No spam, scams, misleading claims, or repeated promotions.
  • Never publish passwords, phone numbers, account details, private member records, or confidential financial information.
  • Share honest experiences. Do not impersonate others or post information you know is false.
  • Use Report if content breaks the rules. Repeated abuse may lead to content being hidden or account restrictions.

Posts and replies are checked for common bad words and spam patterns. Automated checks are not perfect, so members can report content for moderator review.
