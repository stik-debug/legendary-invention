Install ChamaPay on your phone

It opens like an app, with no app store needed.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open ChamaPay in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen) and confirm.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open ChamaPay in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Computer

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
