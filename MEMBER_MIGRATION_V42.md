# Moving a member list into ChamaPro

1. Open the chama Home page as the Chama Admin.
2. Choose **Import CSV**.
3. Upload a UTF-8 CSV or paste the rows. Required headers: `name,phone`; optional header: `role`.
4. Review the preview, then confirm the import.
5. Privately deliver each displayed one-time join code to the corresponding member. Do not share screenshots of the results publicly.
6. Review failed rows and correct them before retrying.
7. Export a copy of the resulting roster using **Export members**.

Allowed bulk-import roles are MEMBER, TREASURER, SECRETARY, and AUDITOR. Assign CHAMA_ADMIN separately after verifying the person. This feature imports membership only and does not change balances, contributions, loans, repayments, or payouts.
