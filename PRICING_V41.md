# ChamaPay monthly subscription pricing

| Plan | Monthly price paid once by the chama | Maximum active members |
|---|---:|---:|
| Starter | KSh 500 | 20 |
| Growth | KSh 1,500 | 70 |
| Pro | KSh 2,000 | 100 |

The subscription is paid **once for the whole chama**, by an authorized chairperson/admin or treasurer. Members do not pay individually to unlock access. The amount is fixed by the selected tier and does not change with the exact number of active members within that tier. Multiple months can be prepaid in one transaction. Access is unlocked only after the payment provider or authorized manual-payment process confirms payment; a submitted reference alone is not proof of payment.
