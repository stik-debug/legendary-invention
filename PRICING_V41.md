# ChamaPay monthly subscription pricing

| Plan | Monthly price | Maximum members |
|---|---:|---:|
| Starter | KSh 500 | 20 |
| Growth | KSh 1,500 | 70 |
| Pro | KSh 2,000 | 100 |

Prices are per chama, not per member. The database startup migration aligns existing plan records with these values. Payment confirmation must still come from the configured payment provider or an authorized manual-payment process; a submitted reference alone is not proof of payment.
