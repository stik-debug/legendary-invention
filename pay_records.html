Add the money the chama received

Paste the M-Pesa messages the chama received (from the phone or till that gets the money, or lines copied from the M-Pesa statement). When a member pastes their payment message, ChamaPay compares its code and amount with these records. A match is recorded automatically. A message that was edited will not match.

You can paste many at once. Lines such as QGH7XY12AB 1000 also work. Phone numbers are shortened when saved, and balances are not kept.

How the check works

A payment is only recorded automatically when its M-Pesa code and amount are both found in these records. You cannot confirm your own payment with a record you added yourself: another official must approve it. ChamaPay cannot see inside M-Pesa, so these records are only as honest as the people who add them. Every addition is written to the audit log.
