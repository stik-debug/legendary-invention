CSV format

Maximum 500 rows / 256 KB. Required headers: type,date,amount_kes,description. For contributions, also include member (phone or exact member name), and preferably reference. Optional contribution method must be Cash, M-Pesa, or Bank.

Supported types: CONTRIBUTION, EXPENSE, OTHER_INCOME, OPENING_BALANCE (one per chama; description must begin “Opening balance as of …”). Dates must be YYYY-MM-DD. Amounts are KES, not cents.

Opening balance rows must reflect the verified cash/bank position at a specific cutoff date. Import only transactions after that cutoff, or the totals will be double-counted. Loan repayment history, loan principal, and payout records are not imported by this version.
