# ChamaPay v53 — Group subscription pricing

- Starter: KSh 500 per month, up to 20 active members.
- Growth: KSh 1,500 per month, up to 70 active members.
- Pro: KSh 2,000 per month, up to 100 active members.
- One authorized chama official (admin/chairperson or treasurer) pays the total once for the group; members are not individually charged to unlock the app.
- The payment provider confirmation must match the full fixed tier amount multiplied by the selected number of months before access is activated.
- The selected tier's fixed monthly price is shown consistently on the public pricing page, chama creation, subscription page, payment total, owner plan settings and value report.
- Existing subscription records and financial history are preserved; plan prices are enforced by the existing startup migration.
